Must-Try Indian Vegetarian Dishes You Can’t Miss

If you’re a vegetarian and you need to try vegetarian dishes, then there are plenty of variety dishes you could try, such as:

Paneer Specialities (Creamy & Comforting)

Paneer is a popular ingredient used in many Indian vegetarian dishes. Its soft texture and mild taste make it perfect for creamy, flavourful curries.

  • Paneer Butter Masala – Creamy tomato gravy with gentle spices.
  • Palak Paneer – Spinach blended smooth with soft paneer cubes.
  • Shahi Paneer – Slightly sweet, rich, and full of flavour.

These classic paneer dishes remain guest favourites at Delhi Brasserie.

Classic Vegetarian Curry London Diners Love

If you’re in the mood for something warm and comforting, a curry is always a good idea. A proper vegetarian curry London diners enjoy is full of balanced spices and slow-cooked flavour.

Some favourites include:

  • Dal Tadka: Yellow lentils seasoned with garlic and cumin.
  • Dal Makhani: Black lentils simmered slowly to develop a smooth, rich, and hearty flavour.
  • Aloo Gobi: Potatoes and cauliflower seasoned simply.
  • Mixed Vegetable Curry: your choice of seasonal vegetables served with thick gravy.

For a delicious traditional vegetarian Indian curry, look no further than Delhi Brasserie. At Delhi Brasserie, we use varying spice blends to prepare all dishes and freshly bought ingredients.

The combination of these types of dishes is why vegetarian Indian food london has become so widely sought after.

Tandoori Starters & Small Plates

Indian meals often begin with something crispy or smoky.

    • Vegetable Samosa – Golden pastry filled with spiced potatoes and peas.
    • Paneer Tikka – Marinated paneer grilled until lightly charred.
    • Tandoori Broccoli – Roasted with spices to provide a very robust taste.

Each of the above starter/petits fours provides evidence that authentic Indian vegetarian food is both pleasurable and very unique in nature.

What Makes Authentic Indian Vegetarian Food Special?

It starts with spices. Whole spices are gently roasted to bring out their aroma. Fresh herbs are added at the right moment. Many dishes are cooked slowly to allow flavours to develop fully.

Different regions of India bring different styles. Some dishes are creamy and mild. Others are bold and spicy. Together, they create variety and balance.

That’s why vegetarian indian food london continues to stand out. It’s not just about removing meat – it’s about celebrating vegetables in the most flavourful way possible.
