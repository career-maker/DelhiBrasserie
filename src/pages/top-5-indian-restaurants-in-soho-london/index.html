by kohinur | Indian Restaurant in Soho | 13 December 2023

Welcome to Soho, the soulful heartland of London’s West End, where the past addresses the present and the streets whisper tales of poets, thespians, and trendsetters. In this vibrant neighborhood, where every block and corner exhibits an achingly beautiful charm, there’s a culinary adventure waiting to unfold—one that’s been evolving since the 1960s.

Soho has a history as diverse as its eclectic culinary scene. Once a posh settlement for the aristocracy, it underwent a delightful transformation in the 19th century. It emerged as the entertainment epicenter of the capital, where art and life converged in a chaotic, creative embrace. And in all the excitement and commotion, one shouldn’t overlook Soho’s dining scene.

Wandering down the winding streets, every turn will reveal a standalone shop, a quaint café, a stylish bar, and, of course, a remarkable restaurant. Soho’s essence is undeniable, from its intricate persona to its relentless pursuit of the next big thing.

Yet, amid the various culinary choices, Indian cuisine in Soho stands proud and unparalleled. It’s a testament to centuries of immigration, cross-cultural fusion, and culinary excellence. Indian restaurants in Soho beckon citizens and tourists alike, offering authentic flavors that transport you from the bustling city of London Soho to the crowded streets of India.

Top 5 Indian Restaurants in Soho You Should Check Out

The beauty of Indian food lies in its layers of flavors, where intricate masalas and ingredients come together and happily coexist. With that in mind, here are ten exciting Indian restaurants in Soho, London that deserve your attention.

1. The Delhi Brasserie

Nestled on Frith Street, The Delhi Brasserie has earned its esteemed reputation for serving authentic Indian dishes in a high-ceilinged, period dining room. This establishment offers classic flavors in an elegant ambiance, making it a go-to spot for those seeking a genuine Indian dining experience.

Founded in May 1985, The Delhi Brasserie has been a stalwart in satisfying the appetites of its customers with a broad range of north Indian cuisine. Located on one of Soho’s most historic streets, this restaurant has become a true Soho institution, sharing the same iconic space with neighbors like Ronnie Scott’s Jazz Club and Bar Italia. If you are a tandoori grill enthusiast, a lover of thali dishes, a meat connoisseur, or want a gluten-free option, The Delhi Brasserie presents various dishes personalized for different palates. It’s a timeless diner preserving the essence of traditional Indian cuisine in an elegant setting.

2.Kricket Soho

Our first stop is Kricket Soho, nestled on Denman Street and famous for its creative selection of Indian-inspired dishes and cocktails. Founded by university friends Will Bowlby and Rik Campbell in 2015, it has evolved from a 20-seater shipping container at Pop Brixton into a perfect diner.

It doesn’t matter if you’re well-versed in Indian cuisine or if this is your first time digging in; the combination of British ingredients and Indian flavors will invigorate your palette. Must-try dishes on the menu highlight crispy Samphire Pakoras and perfectly spiced Keralan Fried Chicken, both Kricket classics you need to take a bite from, along with some fine beverages. The best part? The promised seasonality! Which means the menu is frequently modified, keeping each visit exciting.

3. Gopal’s of Soho

Next, let’s head over to Gopal’s of Soho. A Charming is a family-run diner tucked away on Bateman Street. It has been serving classic Indian flavors for generations. Established in 1988 and still proudly family-run, Gopal’s of Soho is an institution in London’s culinary landscape.

If you’re in the mood for the crisp perfection of Samosas, the creamy indulgence of Chicken Tikka Masala, the flavorful Sheekh Kebabs, or the aromatic depth of Rogan Josh, Gopal’s menu serves it all. And don’t forget to wrap up your meal with the delectable Gulab Jamun.

Over the years, Gopal’s has garnered a collection of prestigious awards and accolades. It’s not just celebrated by the press; it’s a favorite among food guides and, most importantly, loyal customers who keep coming back for more.

4. Masala Zone Soho

Fourth on the list, we are on our way to Masala Zone Soho. Since opening its doors in 2001, Masala Zone Soho has been a culinary darling, earning accolades from prestigious sources such as The Daily Telegraph and Zagat Restaurants Survey Evening Standard, which rate it as one of London’s six best Indian restaurants. The glamorous bangle bar is worth checking out as well, a perfect spot for a sip. It is a colorful Indian eatery decorated with pop and folk art.

Spices are not just ingredients here; they are meticulously selected from India’s best spice farms after the harvest, ensuring authenticity in every dish. With 30 years of exploration into Indian food, the restaurant’s diverse menu offers genuine regional curries, street-inspired starters, traditional thalis, sophisticated grills, and mouth-watering desserts. This makes it a great spot for a true Indian food lover’s paradise.

5. The Kati Roll Company (TKRC)

Last but not least, we have The Kati Roll Company (TKRC), an ode to Kolkata’s beloved street food. Founded by Payal Saha, who brought her nostalgia for these delectable rolls all the way from Kolkata to the bustling streets of Manhattan, TKRC has become a cherished establishment for food lovers seeking a unique culinary experience. Despite starting with no prior restaurant experience, Payal’s passion for delivering the real flavors of India shines through in every bite.

From their halal meats to antibiotic- and hormone-free chicken, the quality is apparent in each roll they serve. The spices are hand-ground, the produce is always fresh, and the marination process is a daily labor of love. Unlike many other eateries, TKRC never resorts to pre-packaged foods or sauces, ensuring that every roll bursts with authentic, homemade flavors. Don’t forget to pair your kati roll with one of their delightful lassi made from house-made organic yogurt and natural flavors. It’s the perfect accompaniment to their iconic kati rolls.

With multiple locations in Manhattan and even one in London, TKRC is the result of the power of one woman’s dedication to preserving the essence of her hometown’s street food, making it a must-visit destination for those in search of a taste of Kolkata in the heart of the Big Apple.

So, there you have it—the top 5 Indian restaurants in Soho, London. Whether you’re a Soho regular or a newcomer, these culinary treasures are waiting to transport you to the vibrant streets of India with every bite. Bon appétit!
