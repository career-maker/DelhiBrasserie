The Magic of Spices

The use of spices is what sets Indian cuisine apart from the rest. Indian cuisine is renowned worldwide for its liberal use of herbs and spices. Turmeric, cumin, coriander, mustard seeds, cardamom, chili, garlic, cloves, saffron, fennel, nutmeg, star anise, and fenugreek are some of the most commonly used spices in Indian cooking. These spices not only add flavor but also have medicinal properties, making them an essential part of any Indian dish.

North Indian Cuisine

North Indian cuisine is distinguished by its thick, tasty gravies. North Indian food is much spicier compared to its southern counterpart. Thanks to its rich aroma and flavor, North Indian cuisine has become a staple in many parts of the world.North Indian cuisine in London is a fusion of the rich and diverse culinary traditions of the northern regions of India and the cosmopolitan and multicultural influences of the British capital.

South Indian Cuisine

On the other hand, South Indian cuisine is known for its light, low calorie appetizing dishes. The cuisine is famous for its inherent flavors and the minimal use of oil and spices. The number of vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes that are a part of the South Indian menu is vast. South Indian cuisine in London is a way of enjoying the diverse and delicious flavors of the southern regions of India in one of the most vibrant and multicultural cities in the world.

Dishes like Idli, Dosa and Sambhar, Uttapam, and Upma are not just popular in India but are recognized and relished globally. Biryani: A Dish Loved by All

Biryani is a flavorful rice dish that’s cooked with aromatic spices and meat (chicken, mutton, or fish), making it a favorite among both North and South Indians. It’s a one-pot dish that’s cooked by layering marinated meat and partially cooked rice, before being sealed and cooked over slow heat. The result is a dish where every grain of rice absorbs the flavors of the meat and spices.

Biryani has numerous regional variants like Hyderabadi Biryani, Beef Biryani , And Mutton Biryani etc., each with their own unique taste and method of preparation.
