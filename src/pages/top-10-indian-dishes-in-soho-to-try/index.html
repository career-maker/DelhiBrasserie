10 Famous Indian Foods in Soho

Are you curious to taste the food that makes the world drool over it? Let’s look at the must-try Indian dishes London that you simply can’t miss.

1. Butter Chicken

Butter chicken is one of the best Indian dishes in Soho. It is a curry made with tender chicken pieces simmered in a rich tomato, butter, and cream sauce. It is famous for its mild spices and aromatic flavor that captures the senses of the foodies.

Butter chicken is always listed among beginner-friendly Indian foods because of its moderate spiciness and customizable cooking.

  • Also known as Murgh Makhani
  • Pairs well with basmati rice and naan

2. Tandoori Lamb Chops

Are you a fan of tandoori chicken? Then here is something you would not want to miss. Tandoori lamb chops are a flavorful meat that rivals even the best tandoori chicken Soho has to offer.

To prepare this food item, specially picked lamb pieces are deeply marinated overnight in yogurt, garlic, ginger, and freshly prepared garam masala and cooked in a clay tandoor oven.

This cooking method gives it a smoky flavor that enhances its texture, aroma, and taste.

  • Smoky, spicy crust with a juicy center
  • Served with grilled onions and mint chutney

3. Kerala Fish Curry

Kerala fish curry in London is a favorite among seafood enthusiasts in the city. It’s a coastal dish made by simmering fresh fish in coconut milk, tamarind, curry leaves, and mustard seeds.

It has a tangy and subtle yet satisfying taste and can be paired well with plain rice and Malabar Paratha.

  • Prepared with white fish like tilapia or sea bass
  • Served with lemon rice or Malabar paratha

4. Paneer Pakora

India is a country with the largest vegetarian population in the world. That is why you’ll find several pure veg dishes from starters to finish in Indian cuisine. Paneer pakora is one of the classic street foods that is also served as a starter in Indian meals.

A crispy, cheesy snack, Paneer Pakora features chunks of Indian cottage cheese dipped in spiced gram flour batter and deep-fried until golden. Do you love paneer tikka Soho? Then you must try paneer pakora.

5. Lamb Peri Peri

Lamb peri peri is a top Indian food London that combines the culinary traditions from Africa, Portugal, and India. Lamb peri peri is not a beginner-friendly dish since it is high in spice level. But this is one of the go-to foods for those who crave spicy, tangy, and addictive flavors.

To cook lamb peri peri, the boneless lamb meat is marinated in a fiery peri peri spice mix for several hours and grilled to lock in the juices.

  • Served sizzling hot with onions and capsicum
  • Order with a side of cucumber raita to tame the fire.

6. Chicken Korma

India is a country with diverse culinary traditions, and the Indian cuisine London introduces them to the Londoners and international visitors to enjoy. The Indian food in London is not limited to the North, South, and Punjabi varieties but also includes Mughlai dishes.

Chicken Korma is a royal food that is cooked in the palace kitchens by expert chefs. Even though most of the Indian curries are very spicy. This one is mild in taste and high in flavor.

  • Braised chicken in a cashew and cream gravy
  • Subtle hints of cardamom and cinnamon
  • Smooth texture and goes well with with butter naan
  • Considered the ‘royalty’ of Mughlai cuisine.

7. Vegetable Kofta

Koftas are spiced vegetable balls made from mashed potatoes, carrots, peas, and paneer, served in a tasty tomato-onion gravy. This vegetarian dish is both hearty and satisfying, perfect for those exploring meat-free Indian dishes in Soho.

Like vegetable kofta, there are other variations available, including malai kofta, nargisi kofta, etc.

  • Best Indian food Soho for first-timers
  • Feels perfect with naan or pulao
  • A satisfying choice for plant-based diners

Did you know? Kofta’s origins trace back to Middle Eastern cuisine, later adapted in India with local ingredients.

8. Biryani Platter

Biryani is an iconic Indian food that is famous and loved by people all over the globe. It is a complete Indian meal that leaves anyone wanting more of it. Biriyani is made of a fragrant mix of basmati rice, saffron, aromatic spices, meat and is slow-cooked to release aroma and flavor.

There are several variations for biryani, and every region has its way of preparing it. From dum biryani and Hyderabadi biryani to Thalassery biryani, this classic dish provides a fulfilling Indian dining experience London.

  • Served with raita and boiled egg on the side.
  • Separate layers give every bite a unique flavor.

Craving some biryani in Soho? Visit the best Indian restaurant in London.

9. Saag Aloo

Searching for a highly nutritious vegan meal in Soho? Then here’s your pick.

Saag Aloo is a traditional North Indian dish that is often paired with mains in the cuisine. But this well-seasoned food is fulfilling enough for a light meal. It is a mix of spinach (saag) and potatoes (aloo), seasoned with cumin, garlic, and turmeric.

10. Gulab Jamun

Gulab jamun is one of the iconic Indian desserts that is perfect to end your meal with a sugary treat. It is soft, milk-based dumplings soaked in rose-scented syrup. In India, no festivals and celebrations are complete without having a piece of gulab jamun.

Some believe that Gulab Jamun was introduced from Persia and is made by the royal chefs of the Mughals. If you’re someone with a sweet tooth, gulab jamun will be your favorite dessert after the first bite.
