What type of food do you serve at your restaurant?

We are an Indian Restaurant serving authentic, freshly prepared Indian food. Mostly North Indian, but we have a selection from South India as well.

The Delhi Brasserie is one of the oldest established Indian Restaurants in the UK. The restaurant opened in May 1985 in Frith Street, Soho, London and remains one of the most popular Indian Restaurants.

How do I make a table reservation?

You can make an Online Booking here on our website – https://delhibrasserie.com/ – or you can call us to make a reservation 020 7437 8261.

It is advisable to make an advanced table booking, especially for weekend dining times. Whilst we welcome those who walk in, at times, you may have to wait for longer periods if you do not book ahead.

Are there vegetarian dishes available?

We have a good selection of vegetarian dishes available; Starters like Aloo Chop, Pakora… Mains like Paneer Makhani, Vegetable Kofta… and side dishes like Daal Tarka, Bhindi Bhaji. And, one not to miss, Vegetarian Thali. You won’t be disappointed with our Vegetarian choices.

How long is my table held for?

All reservations are held for 15 minutes after the reservation time. If you are likely to be late then please call us and we will try to reschedule it.

Does Delhi Brasserie have any awards or reviews?

London Restaurant Guide, Dining Guide, The Diary, Evening Standard, Egon Ronay, Time Out, Lloyds List International Guide, Asian Times, What’s on in London, Asian Express are some of the renowned food critics and publications who have given glowing reviews about our establishment and food.

To read about the reviews, visit https://delhibrasserie.com/reviews/

On Facebook, nearly 13,000 customers have liked us and on Twitter, we have over 3600 followers.

Do you offer Set Menus?

Set Menus are not usually available, although for some special days and holidays, like Valentine’s Day, Christmas & New Year’s Eve etc we may provide some Set Menu options. If and when we do we will announce it on our website and other digital media.

What is the best Indian dish you have? And your Signature dish?

As all of us are different we all have our own taste preferences and favourites. For Tandoori lovers, you can try Tandoori Mixed Grill or Tandoori Prawn Masala, if you like Prawns, for meat lovers, we would recommend Lamb Kata Masala, Chicken Xakuti, for Vegetarians, we would recommend the Daal Tarka, Saag dishes and for one who likes to taste Indian food, try our Veg or Non-Veg Thali.

Do you offer cocktails?

Not right now but we are planning to introduce Delhi Brasserie special cocktails at some time in the future. Watch this space!
